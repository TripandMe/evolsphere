Chapter 3

Designing strategic dashboards

A strong dashboard must satisfy the 5-second rule: a decision-maker must grasp the overall situation, spot the anomaly and identify the root cause at a glance.

3.1 UX/UI principles applied to business intelligence

  • Visual hierarchy: place summary metrics top-left, followed by trend charts and detail below.
  • Color restraint: neutral tones for background and structure, bright colors reserved for threshold alerts.
  • Controlled interactivity: allow drill-down without cluttering the main view.

Chapter 4

Managing human capital and payroll

Payroll is often the largest cost line in service companies and a key competitiveness factor in industry. Managing it demands dual financial and social expertise.

4.1 The 5 canonical effects in social cost control

  1. Level effect: the impact of collective or individual raises on a full-time employee's pay.
  2. Mass effect: the real annual financial impact of those raises given their effective dates.
  3. Carryover effect: the impact of year-N raises on year N+1.
  4. Structure / turnover effect: the gap created by replacing experienced staff with entry-level hires.
  5. Volume effect: the direct impact of headcount changes.

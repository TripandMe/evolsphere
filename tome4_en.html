01

Architecture & principles of the resilient supply chain

The Group's overall competitiveness depends on its ability to turn its logistics chain into an agile, responsive network capable of absorbing market shocks. Models built on static forecasts and massive centralized stock no longer keep pace with the volatility of an omnichannel environment.

Moving to a predictive supply chain requires synchronizing commercial and industrial operations, preventing stock pile-ups while reducing obsolescence costs across the network.

1.2 Ship-from-store & dropshipping

To maximize stock turnover, execution architecture can leverage both central platforms and physical points of sale. Ship-from-store turns every store into a local logistics hub; dropshipping extends the assortment without loading the full stock burden onto the Group.

Central warehouseVolume & standardization · centralized stock · optimized unit costs.
Ship-from-storeProximity & speed · optimized last mile · monetizing store inventory.
DropshippingAssortment & capital · partner-fulfilled shipping · no stock exposure.
02

Twelve SCM maturity milestones

From local sourcing to Net Zero supply.

  1. Local sourcing — short, decentralized, heterogeneous flows.
  2. Consolidation — distribution centers and concentrated receiving.
  3. Lean flows — just-in-time and greater exposure to disruption.
  4. Weather disruptions — vulnerability to shocks and demand spikes.
  5. Dropshipping — assortment extension through direct shipping.
  6. Ship-from-store — stores turned into local logistics hubs.
  1. Integrated S&OP — aligning sales, capacity and operations.
  2. Obsolescence control — bringing overstock and end-of-life items under control.
  3. Predictive SCM — AI and weather-sensitive demand planning.
  4. SCM Risk Radar — early detection of bottlenecks and delays.
  5. WMS framework — standardizing warehouse processes.
  6. Net Zero supply — resilience, unification and full decarbonization.

S&OP & AI feedback

Flow management rests on an S&OP process closed by machine learning, continuously reconciling the sales forecast, warehousing capacity and real logistics execution.

Continuous learning loop

The feedback loop re-tunes replenishment rules based on observed field performance. Automatic decisions must remain governed: critical parameters, exceptions and financial impacts must stay traceable.

Forecast → Confront → Execute → Measure → Learn → Adjust. The loop matters more than the tool.

Chapter 1

Macroeconomic context & margin pressure

Today's economic environment demands a rigorous refocus on operational efficiency. The combination of volatile procurement costs, rising consumer expectations and channel fragmentation is creating an unprecedented "scissor effect" on corporate EBITDA.

1.1 The triple pressure on value

  • Gross margin erosion: the inability to fully pass rising cost-of-goods increases through to consumer prices in real time.
  • IT complexity explosion: the accumulation of heterogeneous software bricks (legacy ERPs, bespoke WMS, CRM modules, standalone web stores) creating decision-making blind spots.
  • The demand for responsiveness: strategic decision timelines that must shrink from several weeks to a few hours.

Chapter 3

Target architecture & the Core Model process

Establishing a standardized Group Core Model is the backbone of the transformation strategy. The goal: define 80% of processes universally, while leaving 20% of controlled flexibility for local or business-specific needs.

3.1 The four pillars of the Core Model

Single source of truthAn item, a customer, a supplier holds exactly one identifier across every system.
Separating regulatory from operationalLocal tax rules never alter the logic of the standard business flow.
Strictly defined approval workflowsRemoving informal email approvals in favor of traceable tools.
API / event-bus integrationReplacing flat-file exchanges with real-time flows.

Chapter 4

The Risk Radar & predictive margin management

The Risk Radar is the central methodological innovation of the Evolsphere collection. Unlike traditional BI dashboards that record losses after the fact, it acts algorithmically before or during execution to block margin erosion.

4.1 The 5 invisible margin leaks

  1. Stacked discount cascades: unauthorized layering of rebates, local promotions and early-payment discounts.
  2. Negative-margin sales: misallocated freight costs on complex orders.
  3. Unprovisioned stock obsolescence: cash tied up in slow-moving SKUs.
  4. Uncollected rebates: forgotten contractual supplier rebate claims.
  5. Logistics billing discrepancies: freight overbilling not reconciled against actual receipts.
Margin risk score (Rt)

Rt = ( Calculated Net-Net Margin − Floor Margin ) × γCustomer × βVolume

If Rt < 0, the order is automatically placed on hold for arbitration by Financial Control before shipment is approved.
